# The badminton team of our school made another great achievement in the general group (final) of the National University

The badminton team of our university achieved remarkable results in the General Group (Finals) of the 26th National University Badminton Championship, held in Qian'an City, Hebei Province, from November 21 to 26, 2024.
The general Group (Finals) of the 26th National University Badminton Championships held in Qian 'an City, Hebei Province from November 21 to 26, 2024 came to a successful conclusion.
With solid skills, tacit cooperation and the spirit of not being afraid of strength, losing and fighting, the badminton team of our university won the third place of the men's team and the fifth place of the women's team in the team competition on November 23 to 24, 2024. On the 25th and 26th, he won the 7th place in the men's singles and the 7th place in the women's singles.
Demonstrating solid skills, seamless teamwork, and an unwavering spirit of fearlessness and determination, the badminton team won the third place in the men's team competition and fifth place in the women's team competition on November 23 and 24, 2024. In the individual events on November 25 and 26, Liao Zhenhong achieved seventh place in the men's singles, and Li Erya secured seventh place in the women's singles, marking further achievements for the team.
In the circular team competition mechanism, all the members of our badminton team and the coach discussed the tactics together, worked together to find the way to win, and made a breakthrough in the finals with clever personnel arrangements, superb skills and unyielding sportsmanship. Even in the face of the key decider many times, the men's team members were still calm and resolute to try to reverse the defeat, reflecting the competitive spirit of the badminton team of our school "the ball does not fall, never give up" and the style of daring to fight and dare to break, and tried to win every point and won the third place.
Under the round-robin competition format, our badminton team members, guided by their coach, collaboratively developed strategies and fought together to secure victories. Through meticulous personnel arrangements, exceptional technical performances, and unyielding sportsmanship, the team excelled in the finals. Despite facing critical decider matches multiple times, the men's team remained composed and tenacious, achieving impressive comebacks. This epitomized the team's motto of "The shuttle never falls; never give up," showcasing their fighting spirit and determination to excel, which ultimately earned them the bronze medal.
The women's group players with only four people to fight, in the case of no replacement, still will be limited to play the maximum effect, the players are not afraid of the strong enemy, go all out, show the spirit of the students of the engineering university, reflect the quality of the sonorous roses, won the fifth place.
In the women's team competition, the players, limited to a four-member squad with no substitutes, maximized their limited resources through strategic arrangements and relentless effort. They faced strong opponents fearlessly, giving their all and embodying the spirit of students from our university. Their resilience and dedication secured a commendable fifth-place finish, reflecting the strength and grace of "sonorous roses."
In the subsequent individual competitions, our school's Liao Zhenhong (men's singles) achieved the seventh place, and Li Erya (women's singles) achieved the seventh place, which added fruitful results for our team this competition.
In addition, the badminton team of our school respects opponents and judges on the court, showing good sports spirit and style, and won the sportsmanship award.
Additionally, the badminton team exhibited exemplary sportsmanship throughout the competition, respecting opponents and referees and demonstrating commendable conduct on the court. For their outstanding performance and sportsmanship, the team was honored with the Sportsmanship Award.
